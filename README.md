# ER-CraftyMerchants-Helper
Helper to setup the Dll Mod designed for compatibility with ANY modpack for Elden ring 1.71.1 I allows you to attach a new kind of "Shop" to any friendly NPC's on your game, a custom/exclusive crafting table separated from the player's own creation menu (you have to use the npc for the crafting) perfect for NPC flavor, or survival like mods.

"Why can't i just turn his shop into a kind of crafting table?" well, now you can, easily add that, CraftyMerchants Helper allows you to scan your modpack and easily add any kind of item and recipe you like, designed for people with no parameter or id experience, or simply those who want to speed things up. it even comes with an experimental function to edit the icons of your items.

DISCLAIMER: the mod and helper itself CANNOT ADD new items for itself, it just uses and scans what you ALREADY HAVE in your vanilla or modded game. if you download the helper you dont need to download the mod, they come as a package
